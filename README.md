**Building trust in healthcare decisions through transparency and verification.**

ProDoc is a centralized digital platform designed to provide patients in Sri Lanka with structured, transparent, and verified data about healthcare professionals. By aggregating qualifications, surgical history, and evidence-backed reviews, ProDoc eliminates the uncertainty of finding the right doctor.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **🛡️ Verified Profiles:** Every doctor's qualifications and practice history are verified against SLMC (Sri Lanka Medical Council) records.  
- **⭐ Evidence-Based Reviews:** A trusted review system where feedback requires proof of consultation (e.g., receipt) to prevent fake ratings.  
- **🤖 Multilingual AI Chatbot:** An intelligent assistant supporting Sinhala, Tamil, and English to guide patients based on symptoms.  
- **🔍 Smart Search:** Filter specialists by hospital, condition, or location with ease.  
- **📱 Responsive Design:** A mobile-first interface built for accessibility across all devices.
